The .pitchpack.json file exported by the pitch analysis tool (or from a session here). It carries the requirements, slide map, pacing, gaps and likely questions.

RFP / tender scope

Pitch deck

PowerPoint (.pptx). Speaker notes are used to infer pacing and who presents which slide. Charts and images are not read yet.

Transcript of the pitch

From Granola or any recorder. Speaker names and timestamps help but are not required. Without timestamps, timing is simply not assessed.
